D (market, Lane B's code, taken over for #37): contracts/Need.schema.json items[] gains optional `allowed_conditions` (array of new, open-box, refurbished, used); an empty or missing array means only `condition`. The market now stores it on `need_item` and `checkLineSpec` and `rankBids` accept any offered condition in it, so open-box lines are eligible for "new or open-box". Why: the buyer's goal sets which conditions are allowed (00-shared Seller model) and the market ranked open-box bids as out. Affects: Lane A (Need writers may send it; `post_need` now takes `allowed_conditions` per item, send [] when there is none), Lane D bridge (sends it).

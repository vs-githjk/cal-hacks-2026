# Pitch (rewritten Fri 21:00 for the PC direction; keep in sync with the artifact)

**The final pitch line is OPEN, Vidyut decides.** The working line below is used everywhere until he rules.

10 seconds: Bidding Royale is the buying agent for PC builders. Tell it your parts and budget: sellers bid, it checks every part fits and every price is real, and you get the best verified deal.

Second beat for sellers (one sentence): sellers see what builders want, what sold, why they lost, and where demand went unfilled.

30 seconds (working draft, every claim marked with its source; nothing here is a result we have not measured):
"Prices for PC parts are wild right now: an RTX 5090 lists at $4,999 against a $1,999 launch price, and 32 GB of DDR5 costs at least $375. Joe is upgrading his PC. His agent already knows the parts he owns, so it never re-buys them. He tells it what he wants and sets an allowance. Sellers bid in a sealed round. A buyer-side advocate throws out the part that does not fit his case, the used part sold as new, and the discount that did not match the price history. Inside his allowance the deal just happens. Above it, a voice agent calls him, reads the deal, and waits for a yes. Not a chatbot: it does the shopping."

Sources for the numbers: plan/research-pc.md section 6 (Capital & Compute citing Tom's Hardware, Sep 1 2026; Tom's Hardware headline). Never say a number for research time, incompatibility rate or return rate: none were found.

Q&A answers to have ready:
- **Why would a seller bid for one order?** Big-box stores will not. Our demo sellers are marketplace or used sellers, or seller agents built from real scraped listings, labelled SIM. For small IT buyers (a school, office or esports cafe buying 10 to 50 PCs), bids on volume are believable. That is the growth path.
- **What do sellers get?** Anonymized demand and trend intelligence: what builders asked for, at what ceiling, including asks no seller met. We do not claim it is valuable yet; it is a hypothesis we test in seller interviews.
- **Who pays?** OPEN, Vidyut decides. Recommended option: builders free, sellers pay a small success fee on won orders.
- **Is it a chatbot?** No. The agent runs the round on its own and calls the builder only above the allowance.

Smell Test

Nothing fishy gets through. Every listing and seller message is screened before the buyer's agent reads it. One that hides instructions or swaps the payee is quarantined with the rules that fired.

Honest numbers only: 13 of 20 held-out attacks stopped on this corpus. Never "secure"; say what it stopped and what it missed.

Voice

Where the wit lives
  • The name, the mark and the Smell Test.
  • One line per screen at most: "Nothing fishy gets through."
  • Empty states may smile: "Quiet waters. No rounds open."
Where it never goes
  • Prices, codes, receipts, approvals and errors: plain and exact.
  • No puns on money, no "catch of the day" on a deal.
  • Never call a real store fishy; say the claim did not match its source.

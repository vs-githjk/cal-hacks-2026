# Customer interviews (PC builders, upgraders, small IT buyers, sellers)
One entry per conversation. Get consent before using a name or quote. Never put a number from an interview on screen without the person's consent. We have no sourced number for research time, compatibility mistakes or return rates, so ask for theirs and record it here.

## PC builder or upgrader script (5 to 10 minutes)
1. What did you last buy or build, and what parts do you already own? Can I see the list or a receipt?
2. Where did you look, how many stores, and how long did it take? (Write down their answer, do not suggest a number.)
3. Has a part ever not fit, arrived not new, or turned out to be a scam or a fake discount? What happened and what did it cost?
4. How do you decide a price is good? Do you check price history?
5. If an agent knew the parts you own, got sellers to bid, and checked fit and price truth, what allowance would you let it buy under without asking? Above that, would a phone call asking yes or no feel right?
6. Would you let it buy a GPU or RAM today? What would stop you?

## Seller or small PC shop script
1. How do you set prices today, and how often do you change them? Could you bid on a single customer's request?
2. What do you wish you knew about what builders want, what they asked for that you could not fill, and why you lose sales?
3. Would you bid through an agent if you only paid on a won order? What fee feels fair?
4. What would you want to know about the buyer, and what must stay private? (We show sellers only aggregates, never an individual.)
5. How do returns and refunds work for you, and what would you want checked at delivery?

## Small IT buyer script (school, office, esports cafe; 10 to 50 PCs)
1. Who buys PCs for you, how often, and how many at a time? Do you ask several sellers for quotes today?
2. What does a bad delivery cost you (wrong model, damaged, late)?
3. Would sealed bids from several sellers, with fit and price checks, be useful? Who has to approve a purchase, and above what amount?

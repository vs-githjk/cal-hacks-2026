# Customer interviews (restaurants, suppliers, students, industry)
One entry per conversation. Get consent before using a name or quote.

| Time | Who (role, place) | Quote | Number they gave | Would they use or pay? | Consent to quote |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Restaurant script
1. Who places your ingredient orders, how often, and from how many suppliers?
2. How long does ordering take each week? How do you decide quantities?
3. When did you last over-order or run out of something? What did it cost?
4. Do you compare prices across suppliers? How?
5. Would you let an agent predict and place the order within limits you set, and tell you after?

## Supplier script
1. How do restaurants order from you today? What does winning a new restaurant cost you?
2. Would you bid on a restaurant's weekly order through an agent if you only paid on won orders?
3. What would you want to know about the buyer, and what must stay private?
